SignToowix Documentation

Everything you need to send, sign and manage documents with SignToowix - a secure, easy-to-use digital document signing platform built for teams of every size. This guide walks through every part of the product in detail: creating and sending documents, managing your Drive, working with templates, and configuring your company's settings.

Getting started

SignToowix is a cloud-based document signing platform. Your organisation's workspace is provisioned by a Super Admin, after which you sign in with your own email and password (or a linked Google account) to start sending and signing documents. Each company runs in its own isolated workspace and database, so your documents, users and settings are never visible to any other company on the platform.

Signing in

  1. Go to sign.toowix.com
  2. Enter your email and password, or click Sign in with Google
  3. If your account has two-factor authentication enabled, enter the verification code emailed to you

Requesting access

If your company doesn't have a workspace yet, use Create Account on the sign-in page and fill in your company name, job title, phone number and the number of users you expect to need. Your request is reviewed by a Super Admin - you'll receive an email the moment it's approved, and can then sign in right away.

Account & sign-in

Forgot your password?

Click Forgot Password on the sign-in page and enter your email. You'll receive a reset link by email - it expires after a period of time for security, so use it promptly.

Signing in with Google

Click Sign in with Google and choose your Google account. First-time users are asked for a few extra details (company name, job title, phone, expected number of users) before their request goes to a Super Admin for approval. Once approved, clicking Sign in with Google again logs you straight in - no password needed.

Two-factor authentication

Turn on 2FA from your Profile page for an extra layer of security. Once enabled, every sign-in requires a one-time code sent to your email in addition to your password.

Sign your-self

Use Sign Yourself when you just need your own signature on a document - no need to send it anywhere. Upload the file, place your signature, initials, date and any other fields, then finish. The signed copy is saved straight to your Documents, complete with a certificate of completion.

Request signatures

Use Request Signatures to send a document to one or more people for signing.

  1. Upload a PDF, Word document, or image
  2. Add each signer's name and email
  3. Drag signature, date, text and other fields onto the document for each signer
  4. Optionally set a signing order, an expiry date, and reminder emails
  5. Click Send - each signer receives an email with a secure signing link

Track progress at any time from Documents → In Progress. You'll be notified by email as each signer completes their part, and again once the document is fully signed.

Widgets in SignToowix

Widgets are the draggable fields you place on a document while preparing it:

  • Signature and Initials - captured by drawing, typing or uploading an image
  • Name, Email, Date and Text - free or auto-filled fields
  • Checkbox and Dropdown - for selections and acknowledgements
  • Stamp - for a company seal or approval stamp

Drag a widget from the left panel onto the page, resize it as needed, then assign it to the signer it belongs to.

Editing your document before sending

Before you click Send, you can reorder pages, delete a page, adjust field position and size, resize text boxes, and preview exactly what each signer will see - all from the same editor, without needing to re-upload the file.

Enforce email OTP verification

Turn on OTP verification for a document to require each signer to enter a one-time code sent to their email before they can open and sign it. This is especially useful for sensitive agreements sent to a public link, or where you want extra assurance the intended recipient is the one signing.

Conditional logic

Show or hide specific fields based on a signer's answer to another field - for example, only showing a "spouse name" field if a signer checks "Married". Set this up from the field's settings while preparing the document, so signers only ever see fields relevant to them.

Guest signing flow

Recipients don't need a SignToowix account to sign. A guest opens the emailed link, verifies their identity (OTP if enabled), reviews the document, completes each field assigned to them, and submits. Once every signer has finished, everyone receives a copy of the final signed PDF along with a certificate of completion.

Create Docs & Folders

From Drive, click New Folder to organise documents and templates, or upload a file directly into any folder. Folders can be nested to mirror how your team organises paperwork, and can be renamed or moved at any time.

Grid vs List view

Switch between a thumbnail Grid view and a compact List view from the toggle in the top-right of Drive, depending on whether you want visual previews of each document or to scan more files at once.

SignToowix™ Drive interface documentation

Drive's toolbar lets you upload a file, create a folder, search by name, sort by date or name, and switch view. Right-click (or use the row's menu) on any item to rename, move, download or delete it. Breadcrumbs at the top always show your current folder path.

Rename documents from SignToowix™ Drive

Select a document in Drive, open its menu, and choose Rename. The new name is reflected everywhere the document appears in your workspace, including in future email notifications - notifications already sent keep the name used at send time.

Need your sign

Documents waiting on your own signature appear here first, so you always know what's blocking on you before anything else in your queue.

In Progress

Documents sent out and still waiting on one or more signers. Open any document to see per-signer status, and send a reminder to whoever hasn't signed yet.

Completed

Every signer has finished. Download the final signed PDF and its certificate of completion from here at any time - both remain available for as long as your workspace retains the document.

Drafts

Documents with fields placed but not yet sent. Pick up a draft any time to finish preparing and send it - nothing is lost if you get interrupted mid-preparation.

Declined

A signer chose to decline rather than sign. You can see their reason (if provided) and resend a corrected document, or reach out to them directly.

Expired

The signing deadline passed before every signer finished. Resend the document to restart the process with a fresh deadline.

Contactbook

Anyone you've sent a document to is automatically saved to your Contactbook, so you don't need to retype their name and email next time you send them something.

Create Templates

Prepare a document with fields as usual, then choose Save as Template instead of Send. The layout and field placement are kept exactly as prepared, ready to reuse whenever you need to send that same kind of document again.

Manage Templates

View, edit, rename or delete your saved templates from Templates. Editing a template only affects future documents sent from it - documents already sent are never changed retroactively.

Use template to create documents

Open a template, click Use Template, add the recipient(s) for this specific send, and click Send - the field layout carries over automatically so you only need to fill in who it's for.

Bulk document send for signature using template

Upload a CSV of recipients against a template to send the same document to many signers at once. Each recipient gets their own individual copy and signing link, and their progress is tracked separately.

Setting up a SignToowix template for public signing

Enable Public Link on a template to get a shareable URL anyone can open and sign, without you sending it to a specific named recipient first - handy for intake forms, waivers and open enrollment documents.

Embedding SignToowix templates

A public template link can be embedded directly in your own website using an iframe, so signers complete it without ever leaving your site.

My Signature

Draw, type or upload your signature and initials from Settings → My Signature. It's reused automatically whenever you sign a document yourself, so you only need to set it up once.

API Token

Generate a personal API token from Settings → API Token to authenticate requests made through the SignToowix API.

Keep it secret: treat your API token like a password. Regenerate it immediately if it is ever exposed.

Webhook

Add a webhook URL to receive real-time notifications - document sent, viewed, signed, completed, declined - as HTTP callbacks to your own server, so you can automate what happens next in your own systems.

Preferences

Set your default timezone, date format and notification preferences from Settings → Preferences, so dates and reminders line up with how your team actually works.

Manage Teams

Group users into teams to organise document ownership and reporting - particularly useful once your company grows beyond a handful of users and needs department-level visibility.

Manage users

Company Admins can add teammates from Settings → Manage Users and assign a role:

  • Admin - full access, including user management and company settings
  • Org Admin - manages an organisation within the company
  • Editor - can create, send and manage documents
  • User - can sign and view documents shared with them

Disable DocumentId

By default, a document ID is printed on the signed PDF's footer for traceability. Turn this off from Settings if you'd rather not have it included in the final file.

Custom email templates

Customise the subject and body of the emails signers and senders receive - invitation, reminder, completion - to match your company's own tone and branding.

Security

Manage two-factor authentication requirements and session behaviour for your company's workspace from Settings → Security. Company Admins can require 2FA for all users in sensitive workspaces.

API overview

SignToowix exposes a REST API for sending documents, checking status and retrieving signed files programmatically. A request looks like this:

curl -X POST https://sign.toowix.com/app/<company>/functions/createdocumentfromapp \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -H "X-Parse-Application-Id: opensign" \
  -H "X-Parse-Session-Token: <your-session-token>" \
  -d '{ "...": "..." }'

Generate a token from Settings → API Token, then contact your administrator if you need the full endpoint reference for your integration.

FAQs

Is my data secure?

Yes. Each company's data is stored in its own isolated database, and all traffic is encrypted in transit.

Can I use this without creating an account?

Yes, if you're only signing a document someone sent you - just open the link in the email, no sign-up required.

What happens if I don't sign before the expiry date?

The document moves to Expired and the sender will need to resend it with a new deadline.

Who do I contact for help?

Reach out to your company's Admin, or email notification@toowix.com.
